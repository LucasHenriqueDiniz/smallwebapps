# Google Account Help — How to download your Google data (Takeout)

> Fetched 2026-11-15. No article date (footer "©2026 Google"). Read for the export guide (slice 6):
> how long an export takes, how long it stays available, file types and splitting.

## What it says

- Time: "Depending on the amount of information in your account, this process could take from a
  few minutes to a few days." "Most people get the link to their archive the same day that they
  request it."
- Notice: "When your archive is created by using one of these options, we'll email you a link to its
  location."
- Expiry: "Your archive expires in about 7 days." "We only allow each archive to be downloaded 5
  times; after that, please request another archive."
- File types: "Zip files" ("These files can be opened on almost any computer.") and "Tgz files" ("You
  may need additional software to open these files on Windows.").
- Size: "Choose the maximum size archive you want to create. If the data you're downloading is larger
  than this size, multiple archives will be created." "Archives that are larger than the size limit
  you selected are split into multiple files." A 50GB limit is mentioned.
- Delivery: "Send download link via email", "Add to Drive", "Add to Dropbox", "Add to Microsoft
  OneDrive", "Add to Box".
- Frequency: "One-time archive" and "Scheduled exports" ("every 2 months for one year").
- Workspace: "Google Workspace administrators can manage if users can download data from different
  products." Advanced Protection: the archive is "scheduled for two days in the future".
- Mobile: "We recommend downloading and viewing Takeout exports from a desktop computer, not a mobile
  device."

## What it does not say

- The size values other than 50GB. Whether an export can be requested from a phone.
- Anything specific to YouTube history.
