# YouTube Help — "Find & share your YouTube Recap"

> Fetched 2026-11-15. No date on the page (footer "©2026 Google"). Read for the recap guide
> (slice 3): eligibility, the period covered, and how long a Recap lasts. Its wording is about the
> 2025 Recap.

## What it says

- What: "Your end of year YouTube Recap brings together the unique interests, creators, music, and
  moments that defined your year on YouTube." "Your Recap includes both long-form videos and YouTube
  Shorts." "Recaps are generated just for you, based on your watch history across YouTube."
- Period: "You'll need enough eligible watch history from January through late October 2025."
  Elsewhere: "Your Recap reflects the content you have watched on YouTube over the past year."
- Not counted: "If your watch history was paused or if you had auto-delete settings turned on, it will
  not count." YouTube TV content and Primetime Channels are excluded.
- Not eligible:
  - "You do not meet the minimum age requirement of 13+ (or the applicable age in your country)."
  - "Supervised accounts are not eligible."
  - "You are not signed-in to YouTube. Delegate users on a Brand account are not eligible."
  - "You are located outside of the eligible regions".
- Where: "You can find your YouTube Recap either in the YouTube mobile app or by using a computer. You
  must be signed in to access your Recap." App: the You tab.
- Lifetime: "No, it is not possible to refresh or edit your Recap." "Once you delete your Recap, you
  won't be able to see it again." "Recaps will be automatically deleted for everyone after the end of
  the year."
- Reach: "Recap is available in over 185+ countries and in 17 languages".
- Music: "you will have a separate YouTube Music Recap available within that app."

## What it does not say

- Whether past years' Recaps exist anywhere after deletion (by its own wording, they do not).
- Whether the rules will be the same for a 2026 Recap.
- Its own date. "January through late October 2025" and "over the past year" sit side by side.
