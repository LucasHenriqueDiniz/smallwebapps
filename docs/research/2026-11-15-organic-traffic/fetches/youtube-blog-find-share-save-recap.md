# YouTube Official Blog — how to find, share and save your YouTube Recap

> Fetched 2026-11-15. Post dated Dec 03, 2025 (Rene Ritchie). Read for the recap guide (slice 3).

## What it says

- "YouTube Recap is currently rolling out globally."
- App: "Open the YouTube app and tap the You tab at the bottom of the screen." Desktop: "Visit
  youtube.com/recap to access your recap directly".
- "Recaps aren't available for children's accounts, supervised accounts, or viewers under the age of
  13." "Award cards are only available in the U.S."
- "Make sure you are signed into your primary YouTube account (the one that has your personal watch
  history)".
- "You can access your recap via the You tab until the end of the year." "Tap the Download button on
  any card to save it to your device."

## What it does not say

- Brand Accounts, paused history, regions other than for Award cards, past years, a 2026 Recap.
