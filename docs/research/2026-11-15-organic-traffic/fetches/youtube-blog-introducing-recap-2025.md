# YouTube Official Blog — "Made for you, by you: Introducing YouTube Recap"

> Fetched 2026-11-15. Post dated Dec 02, 2025. Read for the recap guide (slice 3): when Recap
> launched and what it shows.

## What it says

- Launch: "Recap will be available starting today for YouTube users in North America, and roll out to
  the rest of the world this week."
- Content: "up to 12 different cards" that "spotlight your top channels, interests, and even the
  evolution of your viewing habits, or which personality type you fall into".
- Music: "you'll also see Top Artists and Songs of the year as part of Recap".
- Basis: "moments you explored this year, based on your watch history".
- Where: "Your Recap can be found right on the homepage, or under the "You" tab, accessible on mobile
  devices and desktop." The desktop link points at youtube.com/recap.

## What it does not say

- Who is not eligible, which months count, whether past years can be viewed, how long a Recap stays
  available.
- Anything about a 2026 Recap.
