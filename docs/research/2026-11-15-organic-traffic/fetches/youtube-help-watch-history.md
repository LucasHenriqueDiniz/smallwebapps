# YouTube Help — View, delete, or turn on or off watch history

> Fetched 2026-11-15. No article date. Read for the export guide (slice 6): what a paused history
> leaves out.

## What it says

- "Any videos that you watch while history is turned off won't show up in your history."
- "If you delete some or all of your watch history, YouTube won't base future video recommendations
  on that content."
- "Using DELETE to delete your watch history will also delete your search history from the selected
  time frame."

## What it does not say

- Whether anything is recorded in the background while history is off.
- The auto-delete periods, or their effect beyond "Select your preferred time range".
- Whether deleted history leaves the account entirely.
