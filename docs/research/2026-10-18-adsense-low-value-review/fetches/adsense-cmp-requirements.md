# Google consent management requirements for serving ads in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland (for publishers)

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · the requirement for a Google-certified CMP, read to decide whether the
> site's own consent banner is enough.

## What it says

- Publishers must "use a consent management platform (CMP) that has been certified by Google" that integrates with the IAB TCF.
- Effective 16 January 2024 for the EEA and UK, 31 July 2024 for Switzerland.
- The European regulations messages in "Privacy & messaging" for Ad Manager, AdSense and AdMob are "certified in accordance with the new TCF requirement." A "Google LLC CMP" (TCF CMP ID 300) is listed among certified CMPs.
- "Only traffic from a certified CMP is eligible for personalized ads."
- Traffic from a non-certified CMP "may be eligible for non-personalized ads or limited ads."

## What it does not say

- That a non-certified CMP blocks site approval. The requirement is framed around which ads serve, not around the review.
- How Google's message coexists with a publisher's own banner.
