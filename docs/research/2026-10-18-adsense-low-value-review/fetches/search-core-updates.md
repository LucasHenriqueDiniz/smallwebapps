# Google Search's core updates and your website

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · read for Google's position on whether weak pages drag down a site, and on
> deleting them.

## What it says

- "check if your site overall (not just individual pages) is delivering content that's helpful, reliable, and people first"
- "Deleting content is a last resort, and only to be considered if you think the content can't be salvaged."
- "then deleting the unhelpful content can help the good content on your site perform better."

## What it does not say

- Anything about `noindex` as an alternative to deletion.
- Anything about AdSense.
