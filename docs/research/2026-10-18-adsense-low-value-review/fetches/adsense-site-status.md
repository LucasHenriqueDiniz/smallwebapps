# Check the status of your AdSense sites

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · the page defining the statuses shown in the Sites list, including the
> "Needs attention" status smallwebapps.com carries.

## What it says

- Requires review: "Your site hasn't been checked yet." Requesting review before the setup tasks are done means "your site will not be reviewed and will remain in the \"Getting ready\" status."
- Needs attention: "You need to fix some issues before your site is ready to show ads."
- Getting ready: "We're running some checks on your site." The review "usually takes a few days, but in some cases can take 2-4 weeks."
- Ready: "Your site is ready to show ads."
- On removing and re-adding a site to restart the review: "this can delay the process."

## What it does not say

- Any waiting period between a "Needs attention" verdict and the next review request.
- Any penalty for requesting a review repeatedly.
