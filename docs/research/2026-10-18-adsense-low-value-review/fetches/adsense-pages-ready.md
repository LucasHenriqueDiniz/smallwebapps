# Make sure your site's pages are ready for AdSense

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Google's pre-application checklist, read for navigation and originality
> guidance.

## What it says

- "Google ads may not be placed on sites with scraped or copyrighted content."
- Contribute "your own original content, whether it's specialist knowledge, improvement ideas, reviews, or your personal thoughts."
- Sites need "unique content that's relevant to your visitors and provides a great user experience."
- On navigation it asks about "Alignment", "Readability" and "Functionality", and says "your navigation bar should help users quickly understand how to interact with your site."

## What it does not say

- "Low value content", thin content, or re-review timing. None of these terms appear.
- Anything about traffic.
