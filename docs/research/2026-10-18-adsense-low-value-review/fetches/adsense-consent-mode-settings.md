# Manage consent mode settings

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · read to learn whether AdSense's European regulations message feeds Google
> Consent Mode, which the site already uses through its own banner.

## What it says

- The settings live on the "European regulations message type card" in Privacy & messaging.
- "Google's CMP can interpret your existing EEA, UK, and Swiss user consent choices" for consent mode.
- Enabling the advertising option lets other Google products, such as Google Ads and Google Analytics, use those consent signals. The analytics option applies only when the advertising option is selected.

## What it does not say

- Whether the publisher's own banner must be removed, or may stay for non-EEA traffic.
- Anything about running two consent tools at once.
