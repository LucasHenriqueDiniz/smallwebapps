# Spam policies for Google web search — Doorway abuse

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · read to check whether the size and format variants fit Google's own
> definition of doorway pages, as `indexing.ts` already claims.

## What it says

- "Doorway abuse is when sites or pages are created to rank for specific, similar search queries."
- "They lead users to intermediate pages that aren't as useful as the final destination."
- Examples include "Creating substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy" and "Generating pages to funnel visitors into the actual usable or relevant portion of a site".
- Site reputation section: "Google generally applies a presumption that individual pages (including new pages) match the overall quality of other pages on the domain."

## What it does not say

- This is a Search policy. It says nothing about how AdSense review treats doorway pages.
