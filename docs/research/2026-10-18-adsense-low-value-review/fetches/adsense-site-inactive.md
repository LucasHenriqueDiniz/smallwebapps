# Your site is inactive

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · surfaced by a search for re-review timing. It is about inactive sites, not
> rejected ones, and is kept because it is the only official re-review timeline found.

## What it says

- "If your site remains inactive for over five months then it will need to be reviewed again."
- After the inactivity alert there is "one month to start showing ads again."
- "Before you request a review, make sure you've placed the AdSense code on your site"

## What it does not say

- Anything about "Needs attention", low value content, or how soon a rejected site may be re-submitted.
