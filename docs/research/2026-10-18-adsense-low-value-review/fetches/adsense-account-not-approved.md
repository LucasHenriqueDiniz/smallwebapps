# Your AdSense account wasn't approved

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · Google's own page for rejected applications. Read to learn what the review
> covers and whether pages outside the sign-up URL count.

## What it says

- "Keep in mind that we may review all pages of your site, not just the sign-up URL where you placed the code."
- On low value content: "We believe that there isn't enough original, rich content that would be of value to users."
- "Don't place the ad code on auto-generated pages or pages with little to no original content."
- On insufficient content: "Your site was found to have too little text, and/or your site was deemed to be \"under construction.\"" and "Make sure that your pages have sufficient text".
- On navigation: "Your site should provide a good user experience through clear navigation and organization." and "Provide a clear navigation system for your visitors so that they can click through your pages".
- After fixing: "After you've adjusted your content, sign in to AdSense and confirm that you've resolved the issues on your site."

## What it does not say

- Nothing about `noindex`. It neither excludes `noindex` pages from review nor includes them by name.
- No waiting period and no limit on how often a review can be requested.
- No word count, page count, traffic or domain-age threshold.
