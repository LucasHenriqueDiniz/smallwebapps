# Block Search indexing with noindex

> Fetched 2026-10-18 · read to establish the scope of `noindex`: Search only, or every Google
> product.

## What it says

- With the rule present, "Google will drop that page entirely from Google Search results, regardless of whether other sites link to it."
- "We have to crawl your page in order to see `<meta>` tags and HTTP headers."

## What it does not say

- Anything about AdSense, the Mediapartners-Google crawler, or any product other than Google Search.
